- The main issue is explained as a long-running structural problem rather than a one-off event.

## Topic Threads

### Historical Context

Summarize the long-term background that gives the current event meaning. This section should explain why the series begins where it does and what assumptions the speaker expects the audience to know.

### Mechanism and Causality

Extract the causal chain behind the speaker's argument. Focus on what leads to what, what conditions make the situation unstable, and which variables the speaker considers decisive.

### Cross-Video Evolution

Capture how the series develops over time:

- Which claims are repeated across episodes
- Which points are revised or sharpened later
- Which new variables appear in later parts
